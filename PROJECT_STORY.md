# Rental Platform — Project Story

## My Journey

I came to Auckland, New Zealand in 2024 to pursue a Master's degree in Computer and Information Sciences at AUT. The migration was not easy — especially finding accommodation.

Luckily, I received a 50% accommodation scholarship from AUT and stayed at UniLodge Mayoral Drive. But I quickly discovered the pain points of New Zealand's rental system:

- Most rental websites only let you **view listings** without registering — you need a **NZ mobile number** to create an account
- Some sites let you leave a message, but **no one replies** to overseas applicants
- Facebook group listings felt **unreliable** — were they legitimate rentals or sub-rentals? Would they provide proof of address for a bank account?
- I had very **limited choices**, so I stayed at UniLodge — a small room, but it included everything (rent + utilities) and was good for a first arrival.

After 6 months, my partner joined me and we had to find an apartment near university. That's when I fully experienced how different the NZ rental process was compared to Singapore and Myanmar. Eventually, we found the perfect place at **4E/2 White Street, City Centre**.

## From Personal Experience to Community Help

Since then — while studying and doing part-time jobs (cashier, kitchen helper) — I started helping fellow Burmese community members find their first homes in Auckland. I shared my experience and tips on Facebook groups and my personal profile.

Soon, many people contacted me for help finding apartments, homes, or flatmates before their arrival to New Zealand.

## What I Built (Manually)

I started offering **1-hour rental consultation sessions** (SGD 25). I gather detailed information from each client:

- **Main Tenant:** Name, visa status, university, program, professional work, current location
- **Additional Tenant:** Spouse details, visa, work, location
- **Children:** Name, visa, gender, age
- **Preferences:** Bedrooms needed, budget, move-in date, preferred location

### Services I Provide

1. **Rental Consultation** — Understanding their background, needs, and preferences
2. **Account Setup** — Helping create accounts on sites like Trade Me (which requires NZ mobile + NZ location)
3. **Rental Search Coaching** — How to use rental websites, filter listings, view on map, contact property managers
4. **Viewing on Behalf** — Going to property viewings for overseas clients (SGD 25–35 per viewing depending on location)
   - Take photos and videos
   - Ask questions to property managers
   - Introduce the client to stand out from other applicants
5. **Airport Pickups** — After accommodation is settled, I arrange airport pickups
   - Gather info: adults/children, luggage, flight details, address
   - Find suitable cars (my car, husband's car, friends' cars)
   - Consider child seats (needed for children under 7)
   - Collect driver info: name, contact, car details, license, availability, capacity, pricing
   - Assign available drivers and handle payment (cash in NZ)

### The Manual Pain Points

- I have to save client info in **text files** and schedule meetings in my **Mac Notes**
- Communication is primarily on **WhatsApp** and **Facebook Messenger**
- When clients message me, they don't know if I'm available — I'm **4–5 hours ahead** (timezone difference)
- I manually schedule each viewing in my calendar with address, property manager info, questions to ask
- I track wishlists and viewing lists, update statuses (shortlisted/rejected/selected)
- When I'm unavailable, I ask classmates to go and pay them NZD 20–25 per viewing

## Platform Vision

I want to turn this manual process into a **web application** — a rental platform that:

- Helps people (especially newcomers to NZ) find rental accommodation
- Connects tenants with viewing helpers, airport pickup drivers, and available rooms
- Provides AI-powered rental recommendations, comparisons, and insights
- Grows from free usage to a sustainable platform

### My Role
I am the **admin**, the **property helper**, the **car owner**, and the **home owner** — all roles I play in this ecosystem.

### Goals
- Build a portfolio-worthy application to showcase to recruiters and hiring managers in NZ
- Start with **free usage** to build audience and analyze trends
- Later: AI recommendations, listing comparisons, pros/cons analysis, pickup price estimation
- Monetization model TBD — possibly premium AI features, with a free tier and "buy me a coffee" option
